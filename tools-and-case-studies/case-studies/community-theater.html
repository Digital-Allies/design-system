Case study · Community Theater

COMMUNITY
THEATER.

A digital home for the community theater's 2026 season — not just a site, a system.

The challenge

A working community theater had outgrown its legacy site. Show listings across community productions, indie film screenings, conferences, and youth theater were edited by hand, and nobody but the original builder could safely touch it.

The approach

We rebuilt the site on a dynamic, collection-based architecture — every show, across every category, pulls from one structured event calendar, so a new listing takes minutes, not a developer. A season dashboard tracks attendance and ticket sales by category. Then we trained the staff on the editor, so updates don't wait on us.

What we built

A fast, WCAG-accessible, mobile-first site for the 2026 season — plus a staff playbook so the team runs it themselves going forward.
