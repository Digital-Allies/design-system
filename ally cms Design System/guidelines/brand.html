AllyCMS

Company: Digital Allies CMS Platform

Purpose: A content management system for managing pages, design tokens, and blog publishing across client websites.

Voice & Tone

Clear, direct, and practical. Digital Allies uses its own internal jargon (Departments, Field Notes, Command Center) consistently across all surfaces—admin and public site alike.

Key Values

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), bilingual support (EN/ES), structured data, and a focus on collaboration.
